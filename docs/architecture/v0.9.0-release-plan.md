# kiln v0.9.0 release plan (draft, work in progress)

Status: draft, not approved. The maintainer decides scope. Nothing in this plan is a claim about shipped behaviour. The rivet artifacts named here are `proposed` unless stated otherwise.

The plan is measured against the pulseengine North Star:

> A safety-critical product team can take a WebAssembly component from architecture model to signed code running on a microcontroller, with evidence an assessor can re-check, using open tools only.

Each bet names the objective it serves. Indicator definitions come from pulseengine.eu `data/north-star.toml`, and a bet counts only when that indicator's script reports it.

## Method

1. A planner agent (fable) drafted four bets from:
   - the North Star;
   - rivet's v0.9.0 status;
   - the open issues;
   - the v0.8.0 notes;
   - the seams with other repos.
2. A second agent (fable) checked the draft cold, seeing only the North Star and the plan. For each bet it asked:
   - does the bet move its indicator as defined?
   - can the acceptance criterion fail?
   - is anything overclaimed or busywork?
3. The draft was revised from its findings. The changes are listed at the end.

## Baseline (measured 2026-11-19)

- **v0.9.0 scope in rivet:** 40 artifacts.
  - 5 approved: SR-116, SR-117, SR-66, SR-84, AD-ASYNC-001.
  - 35 proposed.
- **O4 for kiln:** PRs merged from 2026-10-19 to 2026-11-19: 88 by the maintainer, 9 by dependabot, 0 by any other person.
- **O5:** unmeasured. kiln runs a meld-fused wasm32-wasip2 command component (pulseengine.eu's north-star-check) with the same stdout and exit status as wasmtime 50.0.1 on three inputs, but only on the SR-116 branch (PR #693, not yet merged).
- **CI:** hosted runners queued with median waits of 1 to 6 hours this week. There are 9 required checks.

## Bet 1 — the first gate that runs on our own chain (O5)

**Scope.**
- kiln: SR-116 (PR #693) lands.
- kiln CI: a required job runs the pinned north-star-check component on kiln and on wasmtime and fails on any difference in stdout, stderr or exit status. It covers a passing input, two exit-2 inputs, and an input that breaks a rule (exit 1).
- pulseengine.eu: the `north-star-component` job runs the signed component under kiln and deletes `KILN_GAP`. It keeps the equivalence test against the old implementation in CI, and the O5 script publishes the inventory N of tools and gates.

**Indicator.** O5, from unmeasured to 1 of N. That number is valid only once all of these hold:
- the component's signature is verified before it runs;
- the equivalence test against the old implementation runs in CI;
- `KILN_GAP` is gone;
- N is published by the O5 script.

**Acceptance.** The O5 script reports at least 1 on a pulseengine.eu main commit, and kiln's differential job is a required check that is green on main.

**Kill.**
- Any difference in output or exit status between kiln and wasmtime on the pinned component.
- The pulseengine.eu job passes only with a kiln-specific flag or input.
- The flag still exists one varve layer after the release.

**Dependencies.**
1. kiln lands SR-116.
2. kiln is released.
3. The varve layer pins that release.
4. pulseengine.eu flips the job.

The component signature uses the release chain pulseengine.eu already operates.

**Rivet.**
- SR-116 (approved).
- New proposed: the dogfood differential job.

## Bet 2 — kiln as the reference engine for synth's execution match (O1)

**Scope.**
- kiln: a stable embedding surface in kiln-runtime (instantiate, invoke, a trap reported as a numeric code), with an API test.
- kiln: a trap-kind code that the runtime emits at each trap site, unique per spec trap class. It is tested at the trap sites, not through a lookup table.
- kiln: core WAST becomes a ratcheted required check, with the denominator fixed at the 281 vendored files. Excluding a file counts as failing it.
- synth: its oracle pins a kiln release instead of a git revision.

**Indicator.** O1's "execution match on the shipped ELF", from unmeasured to one number reported by synth for one shipped ELF, with the pinned kiln as the reference. That number is synth's to publish. kiln's part is the pinned, trap-coded reference.

**Acceptance.** synth publishes one execution-match number on a shipped ELF that uses the pinned kiln release. kiln's WAST check is required and green at 263 or more of 281, ratcheted.

**Kill.**
- Two spec trap classes share a code at the trap site.
- One trap yields different codes on the `call` and `call_indirect` routes.
- synth has to patch or fork kiln-runtime.
- The fixed denominator shrinks.

**Dependencies.**
1. kiln releases the embedding surface and the trap codes.
2. synth pins that release and reports the number.

## Bet 3 — a proof check that can fail (O3)

**Scope.**
- kiln: the Kani job becomes a per-PR required check, with these named in the plan's rivet artifact:
  - each harness;
  - the live entry path that reaches the function it proves;
  - a ledger of every assumption that narrows a harness's input range (`kani::assume`, unwind bounds, small-typed inputs, stubs), with a committed baseline.
- kiln: a mutation control shows that a change breaking a proven property turns the check red.

**Indicator.** O3: kiln counts as a proof-carrying repository once its proof check, with the ledger, is a required check. The kernel axiom audit the definition also asks for is an open question for Kani-based proofs; the maintainer decides whether to adopt an equivalent or to leave kiln uncounted.

**Acceptance.**
- The Kani job is required and green on main.
- The ledger file exists with its baseline, and CI fails when it grows.
- The mutation control is red on the mutated commit.

**Kill.**
- A harness proves a function with no live caller on the product path.
- The check stays green only after new ledger entries.
- The required job reports green while skipped.

**Dependencies.** Self-hosted runner capacity for Kani.

**Rivet.** SR-103 (proposed) is the base.

## Bet 4 — make outside contribution possible (O4)

**Scope.**
- Adopt the shared CI path classifier (sieve), so docs-only and rivet-only PRs skip heavy jobs. It runs every gate for any path it cannot classify, with a negative control.
- Label ten self-contained issues as ready, each with an acceptance command. Candidates come from the proposed hygiene requirements.

**Indicator.** O4: merged pull requests per month by people other than the maintainer, from 0 (the baseline above). An external contributor is a human account other than the maintainer. Bots and agent-operated accounts do not count. The guard is reverted or reopened work from those pull requests.

**Acceptance.** The O4 script counts at least two such merges in a month after the release, with zero guard events. This cannot gate the v0.9.0 tag; it is assessed one varve layer after release.

**Kill.** Zero external merges within two layers despite ready issues. That would show that access and CI latency are not the bottleneck.

**Unscored hygiene** (moves no North Star indicator):
- the crates.io publish (REQ_CRATES_PUBLISH);
- removing the kilnd alias (SR-66, approved), which leaves `kiln` as the binary Bet 1 runs.

## Order

1. Bet 1.
2. The classifier half of Bet 4, because every later bet waits on CI.
3. Bet 3.
4. Bet 2.

If the release must be cut early, it is cut when Bets 1 and 3 pass, because those move indicators kiln controls. Bet 2's number is synth's to report, and it can follow in a later release.

## Not in v0.9.0

- **SR-84** (P3 async between fused components) and **SR-60** (argument lowering). Both wait on meld artifacts that are not released: the async-edges section and the parameter-area layout. SR-84 is approved, so moving it is the maintainer's decision.
- **The no_std interpreter (REQ_NOSTD_INTERP).** It is a multi-release effort and serves no indicator in this release.
- **kiln itself as a WASI component.** It gets a measurement requirement only. Today the interpreter runs on a spawned large-stack thread.
- **Research items:** REQ_ENGINE_COMPARISON, REQ_ORDEAL_ORACLE, and the external-interpreter half of SR-35.
- **SR-114** (component WAST linking). It has no consumer gate.

## Biggest risk

Throughput. There is one maintainer, and CI latency runs hours. Bets 1 and 2 also need merges in other repos (pulseengine.eu, synth). The mitigation is the order above and the early-cut rule.

## What the cold check changed

- **Bet 1:** acceptance is now the O5 script's output, not a kiln-only job. It requires a verified signature, the equivalence test against the old implementation, and a published N.
- **Bet 2:** it no longer claims an O3 effect. Its acceptance is synth's execution-match number, with a fixed WAST denominator and trap codes tested at the trap sites.
- **Bet 3:**
  - the O2 label and the sigil half are dropped, because kiln is not an O2 consumer repository;
  - the ledger covers every narrowing assumption, not only `kani::assume`;
  - harnesses must name a live entry path;
  - the kernel-axiom-audit gap is stated, not hidden.
- **Bet 4:** the O4 baseline is measured, not presumed. "External contributor" is defined. Publishing and removing kilnd are marked as unscored hygiene.
- **Early-cut rule:** it keeps the two bets that move kiln-controlled indicators.
